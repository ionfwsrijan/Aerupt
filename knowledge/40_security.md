# Security Screening (TSA & Global Rules)

Airport security affects every traveler. These are the standard, widely published rules the KB grounds answers against.

## Liquids 3-1-1 rule (US TSA) / 100 ml rule (EU/global)
- Containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, all fitting in **one clear 1-liter (or quart) bag**.
- One bag per passenger.
- Larger containers go in checked baggage (subject to no flammable restrictions).
- Medical liquids, baby formula, and breast milk are exempt but must be declared and screened separately.

## Electronic devices & batteries
- **Laptops, tablets, and e-readers** must be removed from bags and screened separately in the US (PED screening); many EU airports allow them inside bags with newer scanners. Follow the local lane.
- **Power banks / spare lithium batteries: carry-on only, never in checked bags.** Common limit 100 Wh; 100–160 Wh often allowed with airline approval.
- E-cigarettes and vapes: carry-on only on most carriers.

## What must go in checked or is banned (summary)
- Knives, box cutters, and sharp objects over the allowed size → checked bag.
- Firearms, ammunition, flammables → declared/checked under strict regional rules.
- Gel-type sports balls, large scissors, tools over 7 in → checked.

## ID & boarding
- Adults need a government-issued photo ID for domestic flights; a passport for international travel.
- Some airlines require you to be at the gate 15–30 minutes before departure and at check-in 1–4 hours before.
- Boarding passes are scanned at the gate; your seat, name, and flight are printed/barcoded.

## Programs
- **TSA PreCheck** (US): expedited lane, keep liquids in the bag, no shoe removal. Requires application + background check.
- **Global Entry** (US): expedited customs for international arrivals.
- Equivalent regional programs: e-gates for EU/UK arrivals with biometric passports.

## Compound parameters a traveler asks about
- "Can I bring a power bank in my carry-on?" → Yes, carry-on only, ≤100 Wh typical.
- "How many liquids can I bring?" → 100 ml containers in one clear 1-liter bag per passenger.
- "Do I need to remove my laptop?" → US: yes at security; scannable bags at many EU airports; follow the lane signage.
