# Baggage Allowance

Baggage rules differ by airline and cabin class. This KB covers the rules the booking workflow can ground answers against.

## Carry-on (cabin) baggage
- Typical allowance: **one cabin bag + one personal item** (purse, backpack, laptop bag).
- The EU/US airline standard max size is roughly **55 × 40 × 23 cm (22 × 16 × 9 in)** including handles and wheels.
- Carry-on weight limits vary: budget carriers often allow 7–10 kg; full-service carriers sometimes none, sometimes 10–12 kg.
- Most airlines enforce the size, not the weight, at the gate; oversized bags are checked at the gate.

## Checked baggage
- Economy: usually **one checked bag up to 23 kg** (long-haul). Short-haul/budget flights charge for any checked bag.
- Business/First: typically two checked bags, 23–32 kg each.
- Overweight bags (over 23 kg) incur fees; **over 32 kg may require repacking** and special handling for safety.
- Oversize (sports equipment, instruments) is subject to fees and advance notice.

## Cabin-class differences (typical)
| Class | Carry-on | Checked (long-haul) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy | 1 + personal item | 1 × 23 kg |
| Premium Economy | 1 + personal item | 2 × 23 kg |
| Business | 2 | 2 × 23–32 kg |
| First | 2 + extra | 2 × 32 kg |

## Restricted & special items
- **Power banks and spare lithium batteries must go in carry-on** — never checked; capacity limits apply (commonly 100 Wh per battery, 160 Wh by approval).
- Liquids in carry-on are limited to 100 ml per container and must fit a 1-liter clear bag (see security).
- Musical instruments, strollers, and wheelchairs are handled specially and often fly free of charge but with size limits.
- Electronic cigarettes and any device with a lithium battery: carry-on only on most carriers.
- Food, gifts, and fragile items: protection is at the passenger's risk unless specially packed.

## Booking workflow note
- When AERUPT books a flight, the cabin class in the booking determines the baggage allowance that applies. Passengers with special items should check the operating airline's page.
