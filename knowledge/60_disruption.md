# Disruption: Delays, Cancellations & Rebooking

Operational disruption is a core aviation domain. This KB supports rebooking advice and rights.

## Delay vs cancellation
- A **delay** keeps the flight but shifts the time; the airline often rebooks automatically or allows free rebooking at amenity level.
- A **cancellation** removes the flight; passengers are entitled to rebooking or refund, depending on the fare and rules.

## EU261 / UK261 compensation (Europe)
- Applies to flights departing from the EU/UK, or arriving in the EU/UK on an EU/UK carrier.
- Denied boarding, cancellation, or long delay (3+ hours) → compensation:
  - EUR 250 (≤1,500 km)
  - EUR 400 (1,500–3,500 km intra-EU, or shorter special cases)
  - EUR 600 (>3,500 km)
- Exempt if the event was **extraordinary circumstances** (weather, ATC strikes, security, political unrest).
- Cancellation: right to rebooking or refund + (unless notified 14 days ahead) compensation.
- Duty of care: meals, hotel, and transport during long delays and overnight waits.

## US DOT rules (US)
- No-fee changes: if the airline cancels or delays significantly, the passenger can request a refund or rebooking at no additional cost.
- "Contract of carriage" governs; airlines publish change/rebook policies per disruption type.
- Domestic delay refunds: after 3 hours for most carriers (varies), the airline must refund on request per DOT policy.

## What rebooking normally means
- Rebooked to the **next available flight on the same route** in the same cabin.
- Airlines may reroute via other airports/codies to get you moving sooner.
- If rebooking and refund rules conflict, the traveler chooses one.

## What the ELK-style platform can do for a traveler mid-disruption
1. Locate the booking (reference / flight id).
2. Quote alternate flights on the same route and date (or the traveler's preferred date).
3. Rebook if the fare is changeable; otherwise explain the fare rules.
4. If the ticket is non-refundable and non-changeable, explain the credit path.

## Glossary of disruption terms
| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Irregular operations (IROP) | Any schedule break: delay, cancellation, diversion |
| Misconnect | Missed connection due to a delay on the first leg |
| Involuntary downgrade | Inoperability forcing a lower cabin; refund of fare difference |
| Denied boarding (bumping) | Bumping volunteers/standby when overbooked; compensation applies |
| Aircraft change | Duty swap that may affect seat maps and baggage loads |
