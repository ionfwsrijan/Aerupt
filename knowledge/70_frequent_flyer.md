# Loyalty, Miles & Travel Programs

Frequent-flyer logic is a frequent aviation question. This KB grounds award-redemption and upgrade advice.

## Earning
- Miles accrue on distance or spend, depending on program and fare class.
- Economy and discount fares earn the least; business/first earn the most (e.g., 1.0–3.0×).
- Credit-card partners can add bonus miles per dollar on airline purchases.
- Elite status adds earning multipliers on top.

## Redeeming
- **Award tickets**: book with miles; price varies by saver vs anytime levels.
- Award tickets usually have the same carry-on rules as the cabin they're booked into.
- **Partner awards** cost more miles but add route options.
- Cancel an award → miles redeposited, usually against a **redeposit fee**; some programs are free (US majors largely waived fee post-2020).

## Status & upgrades
- Elite tiers: Silver/Gold/Platinum (naming varies by program); earn status on flown miles/spend.
- Status benefits: priority check-in, lounge access, seat selection, upgrades, extra baggage.
- **Complimentary upgrades**: cleared by status and availability at check-in; operated confirmable upgrades can be paid with miles/cash.

## Practical answers the KB supports
- "Can I book an award flight and cancel it?" → Yes; miles are redeposited minus any redeposit fee; on paid-reflexive fares, refund rules of the fare still apply.
- "Will I keep my free bag?" → Bag allowance follows the cabin booked, not status, on many programs; elite members often get extra/lowered fees.
- "How many miles do I need to Tokyo?" → Saver vs anytime; the exact award chart is program-specific.

## Name-consistency rule
- Miles are non-transferable, and the name on the award ticket must match the frequent-flyer account name (booking platforms enforce this at create-order time).
